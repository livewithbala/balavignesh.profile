Me, specified.

    
If I were a product, this is the spec.

    

      
PRD · Bala Vignesh S · v8.5Status: Shipping

      

        
Problem I solve
Complex business and regulatory needs get lost between stakeholders and engineering, so products ship late or go unused.

        
Target teams
SaaS, PropTech, fintech and HealthTech teams that need a product owner who can own roadmap, delivery and stakeholders end to end.

        
Approach
Research → PRD → prioritize (RICE, MoSCoW) → Scrum delivery → UAT and release sign-off → measure.

        
Success looks like
High adoption, predictable releases, low tech debt and stakeholders who trust the roadmap.

        
Non-goals
Feature factories, roadmaps without a user problem, and compliance as an afterthought.

        
Unfair advantage
A CS engineering and growth background (₹50L→₹1.5Cr/month online revenue) plus ISTQB-certified testing fundamentals, so I speak tech, quality, users and revenue.
